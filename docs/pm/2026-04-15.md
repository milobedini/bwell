## MoSCoW Prioritisation

### Must Have (MVP — Sessions 1-3 Complete)

- **Weekly Goals** — From session 2, every session ends with "what will you do this week?" Patients set behavioural goals (e.g. "go for a walk Tuesday at 4pm"), tick them off for a sense of achievement, and reflect on progress. Ties directly into the Activity Diary for pre-planning. Without this, the session 2-3 homework loop is incomplete. Also required for PWP programs.

### Should Have (Depression Sessions 4-6 — Cognitive Phase)

- **Thinking Traps** — Psychoeducation on cognitive distortions. Patient identifies common traps, reflects on management, then sees solutions. The gateway to the cognitive toolkit. Relatively simple: read/learn mode followed by reflection.
- **Thought Monitoring** — Log unhelpful thoughts, optionally tagging which thinking trap applies. Extends the diary concept. Builds awareness before challenging.
- **Thought Challenging** — Structured evidence worksheet for challenging unhelpful thoughts. Core CBT technique. Completes the cognitive restructuring loop.

### Could Have (Depression Sessions 6-8 — Advanced)

- **Longitudinal Model** — Beck formulation narrative. Patient writes one or two, reflects on understanding. Low frequency, high clinical value but not essential for initial launch.
- **Belief Change Evidence Table** — Two-column evidence for/against beliefs. Multiple beliefs supported.
- **Modifying Unhelpful Beliefs** — Belief modification strategies worksheet.

### Won't Have (This Cycle)

- **Additional programs** (GAD, Panic, OCD, etc.) — Ship Depression first. GAD-7/PDSS are just questionnaires, trivially added once the Depression program proves the model.
- **Self-help program recommendations** — Questionnaire-based suggestions for unguided users. Post-MVP.
- **PWP differentiation** — PWP uses the behavioural subset (PHQ-9, Diary, 5 Areas, Goals, Weekly Goals). Once these are built, PWP is just a filtered view of the CBT toolset. No separate development needed.
- **Admin content authoring UI** — Modules are seeded via scripts. Admin CRUD for programs/modules can wait.
- **Real-time messaging/chat** — Notes on assignments cover therapist-patient communication for now.
- **Data export/reporting** — Nice for therapists but not blocking the core homework loop.

## Recommendation

### 1. Build Weekly Goals (sole priority)

**User value:** This is the last tool needed for a therapist to run sessions 1-3 of the Depression program. It gives patients a structured to-do list for the week, a sense of achievement as they tick items off, and reflection prompts that connect back to their Activity Diary and General Goals.

**Scope from proposal:**
- Goal text entry (what they want to do this week)
- Checkbox/tick-off for achievement tracking
- Optional link to Activity Diary (pre-plan when they'll do it)
- Reflection prompts: "What activities improved your mood?", "What can you take away from this?", "Did you get enough balance across activities this week?", "Did anything get in the way of achieving your goals this week?"
- Therapist review view
- Save-on-tap + submit workflow (consistent with other presenters)

**BE scope:** Model (goal text, completed boolean, optional notes), save/submit handlers, add `weekly_goals` to ModuleType enum in shared-types.

**FE scope:** `WeeklyGoalsPresenter` in `components/attempts/presenters/weekly-goals/`, state hook, therapist review view.

### 2. After Weekly Goals: declare sessions 1-3 MVP complete

With Weekly Goals shipped, the behavioural phase of CBT is fully supported:
- Session 1: PHQ-9 + Activity Diary + Five Areas + General Goals + Reading
- Session 2: PHQ-9 + Activity Diary + Weekly Goals
- Session 3: PHQ-9 + Activity Diary + Weekly Goals (review + refine)

**This is the point to do a real user test with the business partners.** Get a therapist to run three mock sessions using the app before building the cognitive tools.

### 3. Then: begin the cognitive phase (Thinking Traps first)

After sessions 1-3 are validated, Thinking Traps is the natural next step. It introduces the cognitive concepts that Thought Monitoring and Thought Challenging build on.

## Scope Creep Watch

- **Don't add additional programs yet.** Depression first. Other programs share the same tools with different questionnaires.
