## MoSCoW Prioritisation

### Must Have (MVP — Sessions 1-3 Complete)

- **Weekly Goals** — Patients set behavioural goals for the week, tick them off, and reflect on what went well or got in the way. Ties into Activity Diary (pre-plan when goals will happen) and General Goals (weekly breakdown of longer-term goals). Without this, sessions 2-3 have no homework loop, and PWP programs have no behavioural target-setting at all.

### Should Have (Depression Sessions 4-6 — Cognitive Phase)

- **Thinking Traps** — Psychoeducation gateway into the cognitive toolkit. Read/reflect pattern, low build complexity.
- **Thought Monitoring** — Log unhelpful thoughts with optional thinking-trap tagging. Awareness before challenging.
- **Thought Challenging** — Structured evidence worksheet. Core CBT cognitive restructuring technique.

### Could Have (Depression Sessions 6-8 — Advanced)

- **Longitudinal Model** — Beck formulation narrative. High clinical value, low frequency.
- **Belief Change Evidence Table** — Two-column for/against evidence across multiple beliefs.
- **Modifying Unhelpful Beliefs** — Belief modification worksheet.

### Won't Have (This Cycle)

- **Additional programs** (GAD, Panic, OCD, Health Anxiety, Phobias, PTSD, Agoraphobia, Social Anxiety) — Depression must prove the model first. Other programs reuse the same tools with different questionnaires.
- **Self-help recommendations** — Post-MVP.
- **PWP differentiation** — Falls out of the CBT toolset once the behavioural phase is complete.
- **Admin content authoring UI** — Seeds suffice.
- **Messaging/chat** — Notes on assignments are enough for now.
- **Data export** — Not blocking the homework loop.
- **More test coverage (for now)** — Current coverage is sufficient for MVP confidence. Do not spend another cycle on tests before Weekly Goals ships.
